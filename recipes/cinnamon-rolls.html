Cinnamon Rolls

Description

Delicious Swedish pastry.

Ingredients for 25 buns

  • Yeast 35 g
  • Sugar 100 g
  • Milk 300 ml
  • Egg 1
  • Butter 120 g
  • Salt 1 tbsp
  • Ground cardamom 1 tbsp
  • Flour 750 g

Filling

  • Butter 100 g
  • Sugar 50 g
  • Cinnamon 2 tbsp

Glaze (optional)

  • Egg 1
  • Water 2 tbsp
  • Pearl sugar

Steps

  1. Crumble the yeast in a bowl and stir in a few tablespoons of milk.
  2. Melt the butter and pour the milk on it.
  3. Add the rest of the ingredients and knead the dough for 10-15 minutes.
  4. Let the dough rise while covered at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll out the dough so that is about 3 mm thick and 30 cm wide.
  6. Spread the room-temperature butter on top.
  7. Make a mixture of sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle it over the dough.
  8. Roll the dough the long way and cut the roll into 25 pieces.
  9. Place them with the cut edge facing upwards in paper molds, put them on a baking sheet and let rise under a towel for 60 minutes or until the buns have doubled in size.
  10. Beat together the egg and the water and brush the mixture on the buns.
  11. Sprinkel pearl sugar on top and bake in the oven at 220 °C for 5-6 minutes.
