Lasagna

Description

This is a delicious italian dish, made with ragù and pasta sheet, baked in oven.

Ingredients

For the ragù

  • Ground beef 700 g
  • Fresh bacon 270 g
  • Celery 110 g
  • Vegetable broth 350 g
  • Double tomato concentrate 2 tbsp
  • Salt as needed
  • Tomato puree 350 g
  • Carrots 110 g
  • Onions 110 g
  • Red wine 110 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil 6 tbsp
  • Black pepper as needed

For the béchamel sauce

  • Wholi milk 800 g
  • Flour 00 50 g
  • Salt as needed
  • Butter 50 g
  • Nutmeg as needed

For the composition

  • Lasagna sheets 500 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano 150 g
  • Butter as needed

Steps

Ragù

  1. Trim and mince the onions, the celery and the carrots.
  2. Mince the bacon. Add the oil in a casserole.
  3. Once the oil is hot, add the bacon. Cook until the bacon is well browned.
  4. Add the vegetables and cook on low heat, stirring frequently. Turn up the heat and add the minced meat.
  5. Cook for about 10 minutes. Deglaze with the wine and let it evaporate, then add the tomato concentrate.
  6. Add the tomato puree and the hot broth. Keep cooking on low heat and a lid for about 2 hours.
  7. Halfway through cooking add salt and pepper.

Béchamel sauce

  1. Heat the milk in a small saucepan, without boiling it.
  2. In another pan melt the butter.
  3. Add the flour e cook for a few minutes, until you obtain a golden roux.
  4. Add the hot milk in 3 batches, stirring well.
  5. Add salt and nutmeg. Keep stirring until you have a creamy sauce.
  6. Transfer to a small bowl, cover with plastic wrap pressed directly against the surface, and set aside.

Composition

  1. Place a pot full of water on the stove and lightly salt it.
  2. When the water is boiling, add the lasagna sheet one at a time. After 30-40 seconds, drain it.
  3. Take a lasagna pan and put on the bottom a thin layer of béchamel sauce, then on of rahù.
  4. Put a lasagna sheet, a layer of béchamel and one of ragù. Distribute some of the grated Parmigiano.
  5. Put another lasagna sheet and continue in this way for 6 layers.
  6. After the last layer, cover with the remaing ragù and grated Parmigiano.
  7. Add small dollops of butter and bake in a preheated static oven at 170 °C for 40 minutes.
  8. The lasagna is ready!
